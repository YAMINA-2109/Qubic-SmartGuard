## 🎨 Professional Design Patterns

### **Color Palette**

- **Primary**: #3b82f6 (Blue)
- **Secondary**: #8b5cf6 (Purple)
- **Success**: #10b981 (Green)
- **Warning**: #f59e0b (Amber)
- **Error**: #ef4444 (Red)
- **Neutral**: #6b7280 (Gray)

### **Typography**

- **Headers**: 700-800 weight, gradient text
- **Body**: 400-500 weight, high contrast
- **Labels**: 600 weight, uppercase, letter-spacing

### **Spacing System**

- **Base Unit**: 1rem (16px)
- **Card Padding**: 2-2.5rem
- **Section Margins**: 1-2rem
- **Element Spacing**: 0.5-1rem

### **Animation Principles**

- **Duration**: 0.3-0.4s
- **Easing**: cubic-bezier(0.4, 0.0, 0.2, 1)
- **Hover Effects**: translateY(-2px to -8px)
- **Scale**: 1.02 for subtle emphasis

### **Glass Morphism Effects**

- **Backdrop Filter**: blur(10-20px)
- **Background**: rgba(255,255,255,0.05-0.1)
- **Border**: 1-2px rgba(255,255,255,0.1-0.3)
- **Border Radius**: 1-1.5rem
